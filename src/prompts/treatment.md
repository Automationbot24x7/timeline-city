## Decide now (call `submit_treatment`)
1. **style_bible**: your own creative bible. Everything downstream (the script, every image prompt, the music) will use it, not the maker's brief.
   - narrator_persona: who speaks and how: age, warmth, humour, rhythm, how local words are used (2-4 sentences).
   - illustration_style: one reusable style paragraph (at most 60 words) that every image prompt will end with. It must say: no text or lettering, no recognisable real people, faces small or turned away.
   - palette: 4-6 colours as "name #hex", in harmony with the map look.
   - music_direction: instruments, how the mood moves across the film, tempo. Instrumental only, no vocals.
   - title_options: 3-5 short titles, best first. Short, memorable, true. If the brief gives a working title, it is the first option.
2. **stops**: 4-6 stations, in ride order, that carry the story. For each: the era, why it earns a place, the fact ids it will use, and the site ids to light up on the map (from the ride list below). Prefer stops where a curated fact carries the history.
3. **time_direction**: 1-3 honest sentences on how the dates you will use relate to the order of the ride: which leg runs forward in time and which runs backwards. Check it against the dates in the evidence.
4. **arc**: four parts, in order: hook (the first 5 seconds) -> the ride through time -> "they kept coming" (the people who arrived and rebuilt the city) -> the personal last stop ("me and this city"). For each, the purpose and 2-4 beats.
5. **hook**: the opening line idea, at most 10 words, that makes a stranger stay.
6. **kept_coming** and **personal**: which facts carry them and how the personal thread ties back to the ride.
7. **rejected**: facts you considered and dropped, with the reason (accuracy traps, weak sources, off-tone, too much for {{target_seconds}} seconds).
8. **risks**: the accuracy traps you will avoid in the script (read the caveats).

A {{target_seconds}}-second film holds about {{min_words}}-{{max_words}} spoken words, so it can carry perhaps 12-16 facts in total. Choose the strongest, not the most.
