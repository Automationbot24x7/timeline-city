## Rules
- For each scene above, either **rewrite** it or **cut** it.
- A `maker_note` issue is the human maker's review of that scene, not a fact-check failure: rewrite the scene to honour the note, within every rule below and only with facts from the evidence or the maker's personal thread.
- Make the smallest change that makes the scene true. Often only a claim was worded too strongly: then keep the narration and fix the claim. Otherwise soften, attribute ("they say", "by one estimate") or drop the unsupported detail. Do not add new facts, names or numbers, except one to replace a detail you removed.
- Keep its place in the arc and the narrator's warm voice; a correct line can still be beautiful. Respect the word limit given for each scene above.
- Cut only a scene that cannot be saved and that the film does not need. Never cut the title, the end or a personal scene.
- List every factual statement of the new narration as a claim with the source_ids whose excerpt supports it; station names, ride order and distances can cite the map data `osm_ride`; statements from the maker's personal thread are kind "personal" with no sources.
- Update on_screen (headline at most 24 characters, year) if the facts changed.
- No URLs, no living people, no national borders, never "each stop is older than the last". Short sentences for speaking, years as digits, at most one <#0.6#> pause.
- Spoken aloud: no parentheses; no hedges such as "roughly" unless the evidence itself is approximate; name the subject (a place, a court, a building) before using "it", "he" or "the Court". Where a caveat gives the wording ("about half a million", "around 1199"), use that wording. At most one date and one other number per scene: the year card already shows the era.
